How each exam is built

    Every mock exam follows the marks per syllabus area reported for the PeopleCert ITIL Foundation (Version 5) syllabus. Inside the value system area, each exam has 7 guiding principles questions, 2 on governance, 3 on continual improvement, 2 on management practices and 2 on the value system's components.

    Questions mix straight recall with scenario questions, "missing word" questions and "which two" list questions, which are the formats used in ITIL Foundation exams. The random exam draws a fresh set of 40 from all 400 questions using the same weighting.

    These are unofficial practice questions, written to the ITIL Foundation (Version 5) syllabus areas as described publicly in 2026. They are not official exam questions, and the wording of definitions in the official publication may differ. Check the PeopleCert syllabus before your exam.
